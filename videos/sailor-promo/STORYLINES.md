# Storylines

**Picked: A.** Every claim in it can be shown on the real board, and the board is the most striking thing the product has.

## A. "Every effort is a distance" (picked)
- **Device:** a number lands. **Form:** one continuous take that flies across the real race board.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** one class's dhow, close up on a living sea. Each daily item a student earns flies into its points tag, the class total reaches 500, and the camera pulls back so the dhow sails to its first buoy on the real board. Then comes the weekly reveal: the Move Ships button is pressed, the five ships sail one by one, the leader's crown changes hands, and a class reaches جزيرة التحدي with confetti.
- **First 2 s:** a green dhow (slot 3) bobbing large on the turquoise sea, with the question «ماذا لو كان حفظك يحرّك سفينة؟».
- **Beats:** hook → points per student → class total → 500 equals one buoy (pull back) → the map tour → the weekly reveal (payoff) → benefits → claim → logo.
- **Look:** the board's own Living Gulf: sea gradient, foam glints, rope route, Baloo Bhaijaan 2 and Tajawal, with gold only on points and the leader.
- **Sound:** bundled effects on visible actions (pops, a click, whooshes, one impact on the arrival, a sparkle on the logo). No music bed until HeyGen sign-in is available.

## B. "Monday morning" (not built)
A teacher at the registration screen ticks four items for one student, and the projector across the room answers with a ship moving. Two worlds, cut on the click.

## C. "The crown" (not built)
Told only through the leader's crown as it hops between ships across a season, landing on the winner at جزيرة الفوز.
